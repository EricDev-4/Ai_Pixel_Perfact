Style target:
- polished 16-bit / early 32-bit JRPG character sprite style
- lower-fidelity game character art, similar to a classic handheld or SNES-era RPG demake

- strong readable silhouette
- dark outline clusters
- no realistic photo, photorealism, no cinematic render, no 3D model look

Composition:
- exactly one full-body character
- neutral upright pose
- left-facing side view game sprite stance (the game engine mirrors it for the right side)
- any handheld weapon held upright in front of the body, fully visible
- centered
- full body visible from hair to feet
- generous margin on all sides

Background:
- opaque exact flat chroma magenta background #FF00FF
- no gradients, no texture, no shadows, no checkerboard, no faux transparency
